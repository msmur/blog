---
title: "Diary: privacy"
date: "2026-10-06"
---

Diary is a personal journal app. When you turn on Google Drive sync, Diary asks only for access to its own hidden app folder in your Drive (`drive.appdata`). It can't see or change any of your other files.

Diary uses that folder only to keep your diary in sync between your own devices. Your diary never goes to the developer or anyone else. Diary has no servers, analytics or ads.

You can remove Diary's access at any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions), and you can delete its folder from Drive's settings (Manage apps → Diary → Delete hidden app data).
