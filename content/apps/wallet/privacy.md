---
title: "Wallet: privacy"
date: "2026-11-25"
---

Wallet is a personal money tracking app for Android and Mac. Your records, accounts and attachments are stored on your own devices.

When you turn on Google Drive sync, Wallet asks only for access to its own hidden app folder in your Drive (`drive.appdata`). It can't see or change any of your other files. Wallet uses that folder only to keep your wallet in sync between your own devices. Your Google account's email address is kept on your devices only, to show which account the sync uses.

If you let it read a screenshot folder, Wallet reads receipts on your phone with on-device text recognition. Nothing leaves the phone for this. If you also turn on cloud suggestions and enter your own API key, Wallet sends that screenshot to OpenRouter, using your key, to suggest the amount, category and date. This is off until you turn it on, and you choose which apps' screenshots it may send.

To show exchange rates and the prices of what you hold, Wallet fetches public rates and prices. These requests carry only currency codes and the ticker symbols you hold, never your amounts or records. The Prices switch turns price fetching off.

Reminders are scheduled on your device.

Your data never goes to the developer or anyone else. Wallet has no servers, analytics or ads.

You can remove Wallet's access to Google at any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions), and you can delete its sync copy from Drive's settings (Manage apps → Wallet → Delete hidden app data).
