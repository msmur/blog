---
title: "Habits: privacy"
date: "2026-10-28"
---

Habits is a personal habit tracking app. Your habits and check-ins are stored on your phone.

When you turn on Google Drive backup, Habits asks only for access to its own hidden app folder in your Drive (`drive.appdata`). It can't see or change any of your other files. Habits uses that folder only to store backups of your habits, so you can restore them on your own devices. Your Google account's email address is kept on your phone only, to show which account the backups belong to.

If you allow it, Habits reads your step count from Health Connect to track step-based habits. It reads nothing else from Health Connect, and your step data stays on your phone.

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. The error log stays on your phone too.

Your data never goes to the developer or anyone else. Habits has no servers, analytics or ads.

You can remove Habits' access to Google at any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions), and you can delete its backups from Drive's settings (Manage apps → Habits → Delete hidden app data). You can remove its Health Connect access in Health Connect's settings.
